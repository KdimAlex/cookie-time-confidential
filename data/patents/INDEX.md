Sheets

These are public patents and one NASA report. They are not Morehead shop drawings. The plywood Gemini trainer was never patented. A measured drawing, if one exists, would be in Wilson Library finding aid 70177. This folder does not have it.

The cutaway is a reconstruction from the published descriptions. Two barber chairs in the Morehead history, one swiveling base in the later UNC account. It is not a blueprint.

US 1,693,969. Villiger and Bauersfeld, 1928. The Zeiss dumbbell: two star spheres on a shared axis.

US 2,748,652. Carl Zeiss, 1956. Star sphere, lamp, apertured plates, and the two axes. Class anatomy of the projector, not a caliper sheet of Morehead's machine.

US 1,825,462. Link, 1931. The flight trainer. Mercury training used a modified Link trainer.

US 2,364,539. Link, 1944. Celestial navigation trainer.

Kollsman, February 1970. Apollo Optical Subsystem and LM Alignment Optical Telescope, final report. Volume I names the sextant cutaway. Volume II is the figure volume.
